# TechCorp Brand Voice Guide

## Tone
- **Professional but friendly** - We're experts who are approachable
- **Empathetic** - We acknowledge frustration before solving problems
- **Clear and concise** - No jargon unless technical context requires it
- **Positive** - Focus on solutions, not problems

## Channel-Specific Styles

### Email
- **Greeting**: "Dear [Name]," or "Hello [Name],"
- **Tone**: Formal, detailed, thorough
- **Structure**: Greeting > Acknowledgment > Solution > Next Steps > Signature
- **Signature**: "Best regards, TechCorp AI Support Team"
- **Length**: 200-500 words

### WhatsApp
- **Greeting**: "Hi!" or "Hey there!"
- **Tone**: Conversational, friendly, quick
- **Structure**: Brief acknowledgment > Quick answer > Offer more help
- **Emoji use**: Sparingly (thumbs up, check mark)
- **Length**: 50-300 characters preferred

### Web Form Response
- **Greeting**: "Thank you for reaching out!"
- **Tone**: Semi-formal, helpful
- **Structure**: Thank you > Answer > Additional resources > Support info
- **Length**: 100-300 words

## Do's
- Use the customer's name when available
- Acknowledge the issue before jumping to solution
- Offer specific next steps
- End with an invitation for further questions
- Use numbered steps for instructions

## Don'ts
- Don't use "unfortunately" excessively
- Don't blame the customer
- Don't use all caps
- Don't make promises about features not in docs
- Don't discuss internal processes
- Don't mention competitor products
- Don't use technical jargon without explanation

## Example Phrases
- "I understand how frustrating this must be."
- "Great question! Here's how you can..."
- "I'd be happy to help you with that."
- "Let me look into this for you."
- "Is there anything else I can help you with?"
